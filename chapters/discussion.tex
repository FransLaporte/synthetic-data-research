\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\subsection{Integrating the three answers}
\draftnote{Build an evidence-backed mapping: requirement, supporting
studies, metric, required resources, limitations and interpretation.
Trace recommendations to study records, not to the initial candidate list.}

\subsection{Trade-offs and practical choices}
\draftnote{Discuss realism versus useful variation, distribution matching
versus rare-case coverage, overall utility versus subgroup errors, and
assessment cost versus confidence. Explain consequences of missing real
data, labels, suitable networks or priors. Separate findings from proposals.}

\subsection{Debates and research gaps}
\draftnote{Compare contrasting theories and positions, including evidence
about realism, useful variation and downstream utility. Identify what is
unresolved and why it matters. Test the proposal's candidate gap against
existing frameworks and revise it if already addressed; justify a focused
direction for further research from the evidence.}

\subsection{Limitations and future work}
\draftnote{Report search and screening limitations, publication bias,
heterogeneous tasks and measures, and limits of generalisation.
Identify assessments requiring experiments beyond this review.}
