\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Synthetic generation supports LiDAR perception through controlled scenes,
learned scan distributions and hybrid sensor models. RQ1 identifies their
trade-offs in control, coverage, labels and resources; RQ2 shows why sensor
sampling and annotation consistency must accompany geometry. For RQ3,
controlled evaluation on held-out real data is the most direct evidence of
training utility. Distributional metrics, completion results and simulator
testing provide complementary evidence rather than substitutes.
The reviewed studies support targeted supplementation of real data, with
method choice guided by task and available resources. Connecting inexpensive
diagnostics to reliable real-data gains remains a useful research direction.
