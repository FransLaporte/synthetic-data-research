\subsection{Evidence extraction and synthesis}
Use one study record per source, extracted by one author and verified by
the other. Record source locations for each claim. For RQ1, extract method
family, assumptions, control and resource trade-offs. For RQ2, record the
generation task, representation, sensor model, real-data or asset needs,
conditioning, annotation production, scene diversity and compute.
For RQ3, record metrics, reference data, feature networks, train/test splits,
training regime, baselines, uncertainty and evidence on real driving data.

Compare compatible tasks and settings using narrative synthesis and a
method--requirement--evidence matrix. Separate source claims, reported
results and our interpretation. Do not pool reconstruction, generation
and downstream task scores, or results with incompatible datasets, feature
extractors, preprocessing or training budgets. Report missing evidence,
conflicting results and strength of support.

\subsection{Review protocol versus experimental assessment}
This project is a literature review, not a new training experiment.
If an empirical extension is added, specify independent real-data splits,
real-only, synthetic-only and mixed-data baselines, controlled architectures
and budgets, multiple seeds, task and scenario metrics, uncertainty and
safeguards against test-set tuning before running it.
