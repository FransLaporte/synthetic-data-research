\subsection{Extraction and synthesis}
For each focused example, we connect the generation task and representation
to its real-data or asset requirements, sensor modelling, labels and reported
evaluation. Quantitative claims are tied to source tables and their training
regime. We distinguish source findings from our interpretation and proposed
assessment criteria. No effect sizes are pooled across incompatible tasks,
class sets, models or datasets. In particular, bird's-eye-view detection is
not treated as full 3D detection, and binary vehicle segmentation is not
treated as full multiclass semantic segmentation. Uncertainty and resource
information that have not been extracted are left unquantified.
