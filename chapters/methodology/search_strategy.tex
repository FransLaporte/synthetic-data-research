\subsection{Review design and source identification}
This paper is a focused narrative review with a broad-to-LiDAR structure,
not a completed systematic review or a new training experiment. Its evidence
base is the project's shared Zotero collection, the targeted source checks
recorded on 1 October 2026, and follow-up verification of selected LiDAR
papers on 5 October 2026. General surveys and tabular studies establish
conceptual distinctions; LiDARsim, LiDARGen, SynLiDAR and the paired-scenario
study provide the focused technical examples. The follow-up used exact-title
web searches and publisher or author records, followed by targeted reading
of locally available full texts. Search strings and claim locations are
recorded in the repository's research notes.

The earlier protocol proposed searches combining synthetic data and
machine learning, followed by LiDAR, point cloud or range image, generation
or simulation, and autonomous driving. A separate evaluation/transfer query
was intended to avoid excluding generation papers without downstream tests.
Complete database exports and screening totals are not available; those
proposed searches are therefore not represented as completed work.
