\subsection{Review design and search strategy}
The proposed design is a structured scoping review with two stages:
a broad conceptual mapping of synthetic data generation for machine learning,
followed by a focused synthesis of automotive LiDAR generation and evaluation.
The proposed period is 2017 to the final search date, with earlier
foundational sources identified through citation tracing. Record the actual
final search date and any scope changes before final screening.
Candidate databases are IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Scopus and
CVF proceedings, supplemented by citation tracing and arXiv.
Identify preprints separately from peer-reviewed work.

The broad query combines (synthetic data OR data synthesis) AND
(machine learning OR deep learning) AND (generation OR simulation OR survey).
Use this stage to establish method families, not to claim
exhaustive coverage of every application.

The focused query combines (LiDAR OR laser scanning) AND
(point cloud OR range image) AND (synthetic OR generation OR
simulation OR diffusion OR neural rendering) AND
(autonomous driving OR automotive OR driving scene).
Run a companion query for (synthetic-to-real OR domain adaptation
OR evaluation OR detection OR segmentation) with LiDAR and synthetic data.
Do not require an evaluation keyword in every generation search.

Adapt phrase quoting and syntax to each database and pilot recall against
the seed papers. Record exact queries, filters, date, result count and
export location. The initial web-based seed search is exploratory, not
a completed database search or a final set of included studies.
