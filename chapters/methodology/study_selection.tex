\subsection{Scope and strength of evidence}
The synthesis concentrates on sources that explain a generation mechanism,
document automotive LiDAR requirements, or evaluate transfer or simulator
validity. Tabular results are used only for methodological context.
Generation, completion and simulation-for-testing studies remain separate,
and absence of a downstream experiment is treated as a limit on utility
evidence rather than as proof of failure. Publication and preprint versions
of the same work are treated as one study.

The available study notes document AI-assisted extraction, with independent
author screening and verification still pending. Accordingly, the selected
papers form an illustrative evidence set, not an exhaustive or independently
screened corpus. We assess claims by their task, reference data, baselines,
sensor assumptions and reproducibility, rather than assigning an unsupported
numerical quality score.
