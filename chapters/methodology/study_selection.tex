\subsection{Eligibility, screening and study quality}
Proposed inclusion criteria are studies evaluating synthetic visual
training data, relevant evaluation methods, or requirements and assessment
resources for vision tasks. Include domain-general metric papers as
methodological sources, clearly distinguished from direct evidence about
vision training. Exclude unrelated modalities unless that exception
applies, studies without interpretable assessment details, and duplicate
versions. Link related publications to avoid counting evidence twice.

Deduplicate by DOI and title, then screen titles and abstracts before
full texts. Each author will record an independent decision and reason;
discuss disagreements and retain the agreed decision alongside the
original decisions. Report actual counts at each stage and full-text
exclusion reasons.

Assess task relevance, independent real test data, label and subgroup
documentation, baseline comparability, uncertainty and reproducibility.
Record limitations rather than assigning an unexplained total score.
Use these judgements to qualify the strength of the synthesis.
