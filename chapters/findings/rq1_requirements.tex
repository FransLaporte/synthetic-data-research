\subsection{RQ1: Requirements for synthetic vision training data}
\label{sec:rq1}
\subsubsection{Task relevance, realism and similarity}
\draftnote{Investigate geometry, textures, lighting, camera properties,
object boundaries and scene context. Determine when photorealism helps
and when variation matters more. Define the real target distribution;
distinguish matching it from deliberate rare-case oversampling.}

\subsubsection{Coverage and diversity}
\draftnote{Extract requirements for classes, poses, viewpoints, object
sizes, occlusion, environments and rare events. Consider combinations
of conditions, redundancy and dataset size, not only marginal counts.}

\subsubsection{Annotations, bias and provenance}
\draftnote{Investigate label correctness and image--label consistency
for classes, boxes and masks. Examine representation, spurious correlations
and subgroup performance; distinguish imbalance from error disparities.
Extract provenance, reproducibility and applicable privacy requirements.}

\subsubsection{Requirements supported by downstream evidence}
\draftnote{Relate each requirement to real-test utility and robustness.
State conditions, supporting and contradicting studies, and strength of
evidence. Avoid unsupported universal thresholds. End with an answer to RQ1.}
