\subsection{RQ3: Evaluation and transfer to real driving data}
\label{sec:rq3}
\subsubsection{Geometry, distributions and coverage}
\draftnote{Extract geometric distances and range or intensity errors for
paired reconstruction tasks; require meaningful alignment and correspondence.
For unpaired generation, examine distributional metrics, feature-based
distances, diversity and coverage. Record representations, feature networks,
sample sizes and sensitivity to density. Do not assume image FID or a
geometric distance measures downstream LiDAR utility.}

\subsubsection{Labels and real-test perception performance}
\draftnote{Examine annotation consistency, 3D detection AP and segmentation
mIoU under explicitly stated protocols. Compare real-only, synthetic-only
and mixed-data training where reported. Separate gains from extra training
data, domain adaptation, architecture changes and compute. Extract
class-, range- and scenario-specific errors and uncertainty.}

\subsubsection{Assessment resources and validity}
\draftnote{Record real reference scans, labels, sensor metadata, trained
networks, compute and software versions. Check independence of generator
training data, perception training data and real test sequences. Examine
scene overlap, test-set tuning, sensor transfer, reproducibility and
missing baselines. Distinguish reconstruction or completion results from
evidence that generated scenes improve real-world perception.
End with an evidence-qualified answer to RQ3.}
