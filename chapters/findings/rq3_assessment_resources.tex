\subsection{RQ3: Evaluation and real-data transfer}
\label{sec:rq3}
\label{sec:lidar-evaluation}
\textbf{Data-level diagnostics.} Paired geometric or range errors require
aligned, corresponding observations. Unpaired generation instead needs
distributional comparisons that allow legitimate scene variation.
LiDARGen uses \gls{mmd} and \gls{jsd} on
bird's-eye-view (\gls{bev}) histograms and \gls{frd} on RangeNet++ features.
Its KITTI-360 results rank ProjectedGAN better by MMD but LiDARGen better
by FRD and JSD; its nuScenes scans concentrate points near the viewpoint
despite favourable visual comparisons
\parencite{zyrianovLearningGenerateRealistic2022}.
Metric disagreement makes projection, feature network, density and coverage
essential context. No single similarity score establishes training utility.

\textbf{Downstream evidence.} Real-only, synthetic-only, mixed training and
synthetic pretraining followed by real \gls{fine-tuning} answer different
questions. PreSIL reports improved KITTI 3D pedestrian detection from
synthetic pretraining followed by real-data training
\parencite{hurlPreciseSyntheticImage2019}.
However, it reports the best checkpoint on its KITTI evaluation split,
so selection-induced optimism limits comparison with an untouched test set.
LiDARsim instead reports vehicle/background segmentation and BEV detection
on real validation splits. Its KITTI hard BEV score at \gls{iou}~0.7 is
73.7, against 42.2 for modified CARLA and 80.0 for real training; sensor
adaptation and a KITTI-labelled object bank qualify its real-data cost
\parencite{manivasagamLiDARsimRealisticLiDAR2020}.
These are not interchangeable full 3D or multiclass results.

SynLiDAR's augmentation results (Table~\ref{tab:transfer}) show useful
transfer, but PCT's additional gains include \gls{domain-adaptation},
not simply additional samples \parencite{xiaoTransferLearningSynthetic2022}.
LiDARGen's downstream example instead applies an existing segmenter to
densified scans without retraining; it supports completion utility, not
training on unconditional generated scans
\parencite{zyrianovLearningGenerateRealistic2022}.

\textbf{Validity and resources.} Validation supports model selection;
an untouched real test set supports final assessment
\parencite{raschkaModelEvaluationModel2020}.
\Textcite{westphalImprovingModelSelection2019} explicitly address
selection-induced optimism using a multiple-testing correction; their
specialised procedure does not justify ordinary repeated test-set tuning.
For LiDAR, scene or sequence independence must extend to generator inputs
and reconstructed assets. Report sensor calibration, class mappings,
preprocessing, model, training budget, repeated seeds and real-label access.

RQ3 is therefore best answered through task-appropriate real-data
\gls{ap} or mIoU, compared under controlled conditions and broken down by
class, range and scenario. Sensor diagnostics explain discrepancies.
Evaluation of an already-trained autonomy system remains a separate aim:
paired-scenario work finds that aggregate detection precision and recall
need not track planning discrepancies
\parencite{manivasagamZeroDomainGap2023}.
