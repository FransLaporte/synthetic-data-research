\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
This review examines the requirements and assessment of \gls{synthetic-data}
used to train \gls{machine-learning} algorithms for computer vision.
It separates what a dataset should provide, how those properties can be
measured, and what resources are needed to make an assessment meaningful.

\subsection{Motivation and scope}
\draftnote{Develop the motivation with cited evidence about data collection,

Use other modalities as methodological background only, explaining

\begin{researchbox}{Research questions}
\textbf{RQ1 --- Requirements:} What requirements should synthetic data meet
when used to train machine learning algorithms for vision applications?

\textbf{RQ2 --- Metrics:} Which metrics should assess the quality of a
synthetic vision dataset, including realism, coverage, similarity and bias?

\textbf{RQ3 --- Assessment resources:} What real data, statistical prior
knowledge, trained networks and other resources are needed to carry out
these assessments?
\end{researchbox}

\subsection{Contribution and paper structure}
The planned output is an evidence-backed mapping from requirements to
metrics and assessment prerequisites.
Section~\ref{sec:background} defines the concepts;
Section~\ref{sec:methodology} specifies the review procedure;
Section~\ref{sec:findings} organises findings by research question;
Section~\ref{sec:discussion} integrates the answers and discusses limitations.
