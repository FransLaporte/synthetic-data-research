\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
This review examines the requirements and assessment of \gls{synthetic-data}
used to train \gls{machine-learning} algorithms for computer vision.
It separates what a dataset should provide, how those properties can be
measured, and what resources are needed to make an assessment meaningful.
