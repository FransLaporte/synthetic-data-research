\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
Collecting and annotating automotive \gls{lidar} \glspl{point-cloud} is costly,
and uncommon traffic situations are difficult to capture at scale.
\Gls{synthetic-data} offers scene control and generated labels, but differences
from real measurements can limit transfer
\parencite{hurlPreciseSyntheticImage2019,xiaoTransferLearningSynthetic2022}.
Real benchmarks such as KITTI provide calibrated sensor recordings and
object annotations against which perception methods can be assessed
\parencite{geigerAreWeReady2012a}.

This review connects general generation methods to scene-level automotive
LiDAR for \gls{object-detection} and \gls{semantic-segmentation}. It separates
new-scan generation, reconstruction-based simulation and completion of
existing scans. The central concern is whether generated observations
support learning that transfers to real driving data.
