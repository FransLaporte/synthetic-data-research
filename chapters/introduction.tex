\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
This review begins with methods for creating \gls{synthetic-data} for
\gls{machine-learning}, then focuses on LiDAR point clouds for autonomous
driving perception. The broad stage maps generation approaches; the focused
stage examines sensor-specific requirements and usefulness on real data.

\subsection{Motivation and scope}

The general overview covers statistical modelling, procedural and
physics-based simulation, learned generative models, and hybrid approaches.
It is a conceptual mapping, not an exhaustive review of every modality.
The detailed synthesis concerns scene-level automotive LiDAR and training
for 3D object detection and semantic segmentation. Separate new-scene
generation, reconstruction-based novel-view simulation, and completion or
augmentation of existing scans. Object-only shape generation is background
unless a study connects it to driving-scene scans.

\subsection{Contribution and paper structure}
The planned output is a method--requirement--evidence matrix connecting
generation choices to LiDAR properties, evaluation protocols and limitations.
Section~\ref{sec:background} defines the concepts;
Section~\ref{sec:methodology} specifies the review procedure;
Section~\ref{sec:findings} organises findings by research question;
Section~\ref{sec:discussion} integrates the answers and discusses limitations.
