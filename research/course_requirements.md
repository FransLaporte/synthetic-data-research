## Rubric mapping (35 points)

This checklist is not a claim that the criteria have already been met.

| Criterion | Points | Where to develop it | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Review and evaluate literature | 15 | Background, methodology, findings, discussion and research logs | Origins/context/relevance; replicable queries and dates; screening counts/reasons; comparisons of theories and concepts; debates; justified gap. |
| Plan scientific research | 15 | Proposal questions, methodology and plan | Feasible scope; coherent questions; justified methods; grounded expectations; dates and resources; supervisor alignment; ethics and integrity. |
| Scientific writing and argument | 5 | Both documents and bibliography | Logical structure; precise claims; evidence linked to conclusions; interpretation distinguished from opinion; consistent references; accurate visuals. |

## Milestone checks

- Before 5 October: record actual searches and screening; compare sources;
  clearly mark unfinished parts of the draft. Do not invent counts.
- Before 9 October: substantiate the candidate gap using initial literature,
  verify expectations and feasibility, and agree the scope and methods.
- On 12 October: log peer comments, document locations, responses and actions.
- On 19 October: reconcile supervisor and peer feedback; log protocol changes
  and their implications for earlier screening decisions.
- Before 6 November: close or explain feedback actions, reconcile both documents,
  audit citations and claims, and compile and inspect all outputs.
