## Claims and limits

- Section III, dataset format: forward-facing 90-degree point clouds, no reflectance and no motion distortion.
- Simulation section: depth-based sampling avoids simplified game collision geometry.
- Section IV.B and Table II: AVOD-FPN pretraining on synthetic data followed by KITTI fine-tuning improves pedestrian AP in the reported setting.
- Crucial validity qualification: KITTI's labelled training set is split into 3,712 training and 3,769 evaluation instances, and the best evaluated checkpoint is reported for each configuration. This is not an untouched final test.
- No numerical gain is copied into the shortened review; the claim is qualitative and the selection limitation is explicit.
